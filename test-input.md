We just wrapped a tabletop exercise with a mid-size DeFi protocol. Their team assumed their multisig was their security perimeter. Took about 20 minutes to show them three different paths to compromise that never touched the multisig — social engineering the key holders, exploiting a dependency in their deployment pipeline, and a governance attack using flash-loaned voting tokens. The interesting part wasn't the attacks themselves, it was watching their mental model shift in real time. They went from "we're secure because multisig" to "oh, the multisig is one control among many and we have gaps everywhere else." That's the actual value of a tabletop — not the scenarios, but forcing the team to think adversarially about their own system.
